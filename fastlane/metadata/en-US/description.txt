XGlassShot is a lightweight screenshot and recording tool for Mac. Free to download, with no account, subscription, in-app purchase or cloud AI service.

CAPTURE ON THE DISPLAY YOU CHOOSE
Select an area for a screenshot or video recording. Use Standard 1× or Retina 2× image quality. Default shortcuts: ⌘⇧2 for screenshots and ⌘⇧1 for recordings. Change or disable screenshot, recording, quality and filename-prefix shortcuts in Settings > Shortcuts.

YOUR FOLDER. YOUR FILENAMES.
Choose a save folder and customize the filename prefix, including Unicode and spaces. Automatic captures receive unique names and never overwrite an existing file. An optional save panel gives you control for each capture.

LOCAL IMAGE TOOLS
Copy images, recognize Chinese and English text with Apple Vision, or add a background, padding and rounded corners in the beautification editor. These operations stay on your Mac.

A COMPACT MAC WORKSPACE
Choose Day, Night or Follow System. Import your own background image and adjust its transparency while keeping the actual screenshot clear. Keyboard navigation and native controls fit your Mac workflow.

OPEN SOURCE AND AGENT-FRIENDLY
The MIT-licensed source includes a separate JSON command-line tool for scripts and agents. The CLI is built separately from the store app; setup instructions are on the project website.

Requires macOS 14.6 or later and Screen Recording permission for capture. Recordings currently contain video only. No analytics, advertising or tracking.

Privacy policy

XGlassShot for macOS · Effective October 18, 2026

XGlassShot processes screen captures and text locally. The app does not collect or transmit personal data, use analytics, display advertising, track users or contact a cloud AI service. No account is required. The distributed app does not enable outgoing network connections.

Screen content and permissions

Screen Recording permission lets you capture the area and display you select. Images and video are saved to your chosen local folder. Text recognition uses Apple Vision on your Mac; styling and previews also run locally. Audio capture is not included.

Files, settings and clipboard

Settings and folder-access bookmarks are stored locally in the app sandbox. Imported background images are copied locally and may be removed in Settings → Appearance. Unfinished or unsaved recordings may remain in the recovery folder to prevent data loss; open it with Recover Recordings… and remove files you no longer need.

Copy actions write an image or recognized text to the macOS clipboard. Other apps and Apple's clipboard features may access or synchronize it according to your system settings. Files saved to a synchronized folder may sync through the service you chose; XGlassShot does not operate that service.

Your control

You can delete captures and recovery files, replace or remove backgrounds, choose a different folder, and revoke Screen Recording permission in System Settings. The optional command-line tool uses its own calling terminal's permissions and explicit file destinations.

Support and website

If you choose to submit a GitHub issue, GitHub receives the information you provide under its own privacy policy. Do not include private screenshots or credentials. This documentation site is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses no application analytics or cookies of its own; GitHub may process ordinary hosting request logs under its policy.

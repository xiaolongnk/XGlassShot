GlassCap is a modern screen capture app for Mac. Record a region or take a screenshot with a single shortcut—no menus, no clutter. Built with Apple's ScreenCaptureKit and SwiftUI for high quality and a clean, glass-style interface.

Capture • Select any area on screen and capture it as an image or start a video recording.

Shortcuts • ⌘⇧2 for screenshot, ⌘⇧1 for record. Change save path (⌘⇧P) and filename suffix (⌘⇧L) without leaving your workflow.

Settings dock • Pick save location, toggle Low/High quality, optional capture sound and screenshot border, launch at login.

Multi-monitor • Accurate selection and capture on every display.

Requires macOS 12.3 or later and Screen Recording permission.
